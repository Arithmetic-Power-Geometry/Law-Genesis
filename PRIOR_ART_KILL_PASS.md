## 1. Generated congruence and quotient machinery

The operation "take a relation and close it to the least congruence compatible with the algebraic operations" is standard universal-algebraic machinery. Law Genesis therefore does not claim novelty for generated congruences, quotient algebras, or congruence closure algorithms themselves.

The framework-specific object is narrower: microscopic law disagreement first determines a raw relation `R_F`; dynamical compatibility then forces the least common-law congruence `Theta_F`; finally the information cost of that forced quotient is measured by `Gamma`.

Safe claim: the contribution is the coupling

`raw law disagreement -> least dynamically compatible common-law congruence -> information cost`,

plus separation results showing that low-order disagreement summaries need not determine the resulting Law Genesis Cost.

## 2. Automata minimization and partition refinement

Automata minimization, observational congruence, bisimulation, and generic partition refinement already compute stable equivalence classes compatible with transitions. Modern treatments explicitly formulate minimization through congruence/partition refinement, and generic partition-refinement methods cover deterministic, weighted, probabilistic, and other transition systems.

Law Genesis therefore must not claim that refining a partition until transition compatibility is reached is new.

Residual distinction: the Law Genesis problem starts from *multiple competing microscopic laws that are required to descend to one and the same effective law*. The raw relation is generated by pointwise law disagreement across that family, and `Gamma` quantifies the information destroyed by the least compatible quotient. This framing should be presented as a new problem formulation and measurement layer unless a stronger equivalence to a standard automata-minimization construction is later proved.

## 3. Transformation semigroups and synchronizing automata

Finite transformation semigroups already study actions of transformations on finite sets, congruences, primitive actions, words in transition monoids, synchronization, reset thresholds, and permutation/non-permutation interactions. Synchronizing-automata theory studies how words collapse state sets, and permutation letters are a standard structural ingredient in circular and completely reachable automata.

Law Genesis therefore must not claim novelty for transformation words, permutation interventions, delayed effects of words, ranks/images of words, or synchronization depth as such.

Residual distinction: the current intrinsic witness does not ask whether a word synchronizes a single automaton. It compares two *families of microscopic laws* under the same intervention word and asks when the least quotient supporting one common descended law acquires a different information cost. This is presently best treated as a Law-Genesis-specific observable on transformation dynamics, not as a new semigroup mechanism.

## 4. Bisimulation and quotient transition systems

Bisimulation and related behavioral equivalences provide exact quotient constructions for transition systems. Forward/backward bisimulations and related aggregation notions are well established across probabilistic systems, chemical reaction networks, rule-based systems, and other dynamical models.

Law Genesis therefore must not claim novelty for the general idea of behavioral equivalence, quotient transition systems, or exact aggregation by compatible partitions.

Residual distinction: Law Genesis fixes a special forcing condition—different microscopic laws must become one effective law after quotienting—and measures the minimal information loss required to achieve that condition. The fixed raw-disagreement separation theorem shows that even the same pointwise disagreement relation can induce different least compatible abstractions when propagated through different microscopic-law families.

## 5. Lumpability and aggregation

Exact and ordinary lumpability are classical state-aggregation concepts for Markov chains. Recent work continues to optimize exact aggregation and compare reduced chains. Lumpability is therefore direct prior art for any broad claim that a partition can preserve macro-dynamics.

Law Genesis therefore must not claim novelty for exact aggregation, macro-state partitions, or information-theoretic comparison of reduced systems in general.

Residual distinction: the present finite deterministic theory is not a probabilistic lumpability criterion. Its defining constraint is simultaneous descent of several deterministic microscopic laws to one map, followed by an entropy cost on the forced quotient. A future probabilistic Law Genesis extension would need a separate novelty analysis against lumpability and quasi-lumpability.

## 6. Linear reduction boundary

In the linear setting, the least kernel supporting one descended law is exactly the smallest invariant/reachable subspace generated by the law-difference images. This is classical control/reachability theory.

This is a reduction result, not a novelty claim. The paper should state it prominently because it sharply identifies where the nonlinear finite theory begins.

## 7. Strongest surviving claims after the kill pass

The following claims survive as the strongest Law-Genesis-specific statements presently supported by the repository:

1. **Common-Law Congruence Representation.** The finest quotient on which all microscopic laws descend to one effective law is the quotient by the least congruence containing all pointwise law disagreements. The generated-congruence mechanism is standard; the theorem is the exact representation of the Law Genesis constraint.

2. **Law Genesis Cost.** `Gamma(F)=H(X|X/Theta_F)` measures the information cost of enforcing one reusable effective law on a finite deterministic law family.

3. **Gamma/fragility separation.** Equal Law Genesis Cost does not determine critical seed rank or the geometry of forced abstraction.

4. **Fixed Raw-Disagreement Separation.** There exist two finite three-law systems with exactly the same raw disagreement relation but different least common-law congruences and different Law Genesis Costs. This is an exact finite structural theorem.

5. **Clock-free intrinsic sequential witness.** There exist two four-state three-law systems under the same permutation intervention that match multiple low-depth Law-Genesis observables through depths 0,1,2 and separate in `Gamma` at depth 3. This is a deterministic computational witness, not yet a general theorem.

6. **Arbitrary-delay separation.** Law-Genesis separation can be postponed to any prescribed finite depth by a delay lift. This is exact, but the delay mechanism itself is standard-style machinery and is not the flagship novelty claim.

## 8. Claims that should be avoided

Do not claim:

- invention of congruence closure;
- invention of quotient dynamics or behavioral equivalence;
- invention of automata partition refinement;
- invention of transformation-semigroup depth effects;
- invention of synchronizing words or reset thresholds;
- invention of lumpability or exact aggregation;
- a new linear reachability mechanism;
- that the current four-state witness proves an arbitrary-depth intrinsic theorem;
- that a finite computational witness alone establishes world-first novelty.

## 9. Manuscript novelty sentence

A safe high-level formulation is:

> Law Genesis studies the minimum information that must be discarded so that several microscopic deterministic laws can be represented by one reusable effective law, and separates the amount of microscopic disagreement from the dynamically forced abstraction generated by that disagreement.

The flagship structural message is:

`same microscopic disagreement does not imply the same forced common-law abstraction`.

The flagship sequential message is:

`agreement on rich low-depth Law-Genesis observables does not necessarily determine later common-law cost`.
